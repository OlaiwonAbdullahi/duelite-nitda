# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Nigerian students and course representatives managing departmental dues. The dashboard is a frontend demonstration for presenters and reviewers.

## Product Purpose
Help representatives register, prepare class spaces and collect dues with transparent approval, verification and withdrawal safeguards. The milestone plan in local handoff.md defines implementation scope.

## Brand Personality
Simple, clear and accountable: preserve the existing Duelite identity and landing-page tokens.

## Anti-references
Do not imply real banking, identity verification or live financial activity in this demo. Avoid decorative dashboard metrics that imply collections already exist.

## Design Principles
- Make the next permitted action clear.
- Explain approval and verification separately.
- Keep student access additive to representative access.
- Label simulated outcomes and presenter controls explicitly.
- Preserve existing brand conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive mobile and desktop layouts, keyboard navigation, labelled fields, readable contrast, accessible progress and errors, and reduced-motion support, as required by the milestone plan.
